One of the three categories of devil fruits is Logia, which allow the user to transform into and generate an element of nature. Not to be mistaken with some Paramecia or Zoan fruits that have similar properties but don’t fulfill the Logia requirements. It’s unknown what Logia awakening consists of, but some Logia users have been able to achieve a stage where they can make their elements come to life. Gasu Gasu no Mi, or Gas Gas Fruit Allows the user to transform into the natural element of gases, able to freely generate and turn into gaseous substances. The user can transform into a gaseous form that cannot be grabbed without haki and can fly around, as well as generate and interact with other types of gases, which can be poisonous, flammable, or have other effects. The user can even manipulate the gas that is oxygen, causing others to suffocate. Eaten of Caesar Clown. Goro Goro no Mi, or Thunder Thunder Fruit - Turns you into a Lightningman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of lightning, able to freely generate and turn into electricity, move around at the speed of lightning, or smite anything with thunder, so long as the target can conduct electricity, making this quite a dangerous fruit with incredible firepower. With advanced enough technology, the lightning can even be used to power some machinery. Eaten by Enel, who has advanced it to the point of being able to make it come to life. Gunyo Gunyo no Mi, or Gloop Gloop Fruit - Evolves you into a Clayman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of clay, able to freely generate and turn into gloopy clay. This clay can then be shaped to create golems. Eaten by Prince Grus, who has advanced it to the point of being able to make it come to life by making clay golems, though while he seemed to transform his hand into the element, it’s unknown if this could actually be a Paramecia fruit that creates living creatures rather than a Logia. Hie Hie no Mi, or Cold Cold Fruit - Evolves you into a Freezeman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of ice, able to freely generate and turn into solid ice themselves. They can use this ice to freeze other things or people solid. Someone frozen solid becomes vulnerable to being shattered more easily, and if not defrosted, will die after not long. A skilled enough user can freeze entire large surfaces in moments. Eaten by Kuzan, or former Admiral Aokiji, who has advanced it to the point of being able to make it come to life. Magu Magu no Mi, or Magma Magma Fruit - Evolves you into a Magmaman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of magma, able to freely generate and turn into semiliquid magma. This magma has incredible scorching power, able to burn and eviscerate anything it touches, having disintegrated even some of the most durable substances known. Eaten by Sakazuki, or Admiral Akainu, who has advanced it to the point of being able to make it come to life. Mera Mera no Mi, or Flame Flame Fruit Allows the user to transform into the natural element of fire, able to freely generate and turn into scorching flames. Also provides the user with immunity to temperatures due to always being warm. Countered by elements that put out fire. Eaten by Portgas D. Ace, and later eaten by Sabo. Moku Moku no Mi, or Smoke Smoke Fruit - Turns you into a Smokeman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of smoke, able to freely generate and turn into gaseous smoke. It also seemingly makes the user immune to the damages of smoking due to being made of smoke. Eaten by Smoker. Mori Mori no Mi, or Woods Woods Fruit - Evolves you into a Forestman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of flora, able to freely generate and turn into any type of plant, flower, herb, or tree among other flora. This can allow the user to generate living plants in areas that may otherwise be deserted, as well as control them freely, resulting in vines and trunks that can attack their targets. The user can dehydrate others by sucking their nutrients through roots, and also doesn’t require nutrition as long as they are in contact with the sun. Eaten by Aramaki, or Admiral Ryokugyu. Numa Numa no Mi, or Swamp Swamp Fruit - Evolves you into a Swampman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of swampsoil, able to freely generate and turn into sticky semiliquid swamps. These swamps act like moving sands, in they can sink anything or anyone they want into them. The user can then absorb those who have fallen into the swamps into their own body, where they’ll remain intact within the depths of the swamp like a pocket dimension. Living beings can be stored inside while left unconscious and survive, but not for too extensive periods of time. Eaten by Caribou. Pika Pika no Mi, or Light Light Fruit - Evolves you into a Lightman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of light, able to freely generate and turn into light particles. The user can use the light’s laser beam-like properties to attack targets and cause explosions, and can also move at the speed of light, making them incredibly quick and lethal. Eaten by Borsalino, or Admiral Kizaru, who has advanced the fruit enough to the point of being able to turn its individual particles into life, making them his clones. Suna Suna no Mi, or Sand Sand Fruit - Turns you into a Sandman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of sand, able to freely generate and turn into grains of sand. Through it, they can also absorb the moisture out of people and things, drying and dehydrating their targets, other items, or entire areas. When hardened with a liquid, the user will not be able to dissipate anymore as their sand hardens. Eaten by Crocodile. Susu Susu no Mi, or Soot Soot Fruit Allows the user to transform into the natural element of soot, able to freely generate and turn into powdery soot. This soot can harden into shapes that can be used to hurt targets. Eaten by Karasu, who has advanced it to the point of being able to make it come to life. Yami Yami no Mi, or Dark Dark Fruit - Evolves you into a Darknessman Allows the user to manipulate the natural element of darkness itself, able to freely generate pitch-black darkness. Unlike regular Logia users, the user cannot transform into the element and will thus receive any attack, which has led many to debate whether this fruit truly is a Logia, or yet another coverup by the Government, but the fruit’s elusiveness makes this hard to determine. The darkness generated by the user acts as a gravitational force, able to draw other things in like a black hole. This fruit also has the very peculiar property of being able to generate black water that can neutralize the powers of devil fruit users, once again raising suspicion towards the fruit’s true nature. Eaten by Blackbeard, who had been hunting for the fruit for over two decades. Yuki Yuki no Mi, or Snow Snow Fruit - Evolves you into a Snowman Allows the user to transform into the natural element of snow, able to freely generate and turn into powdery snow. The snow can be made to be soft or quite hard and layered, though not as durable as some other substances. Thanks to their powers, the user can generate cold around their targets with their snow, but aren’t vulnerable to heat or cold themselves. Eaten by Monet.
